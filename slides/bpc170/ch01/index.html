The method

Six steps turn panic into process.

1Identify

Gather evidence first.

2Theorize

Rank probable causes.

3Test

Prove or cut each theory.

4Act

Plan the fix, then apply it.

5Verify

Confirm full function.

6Document

Write it down for the next tech.

CompTIA’s best-practice method. When your company has its own process, company policy wins.

BPC170 · 1.2
Step 1 · Identify

Collect evidence before you touch anything.

  • What exactly fails?No image. Power light on. Monitor LED amber.
  • Who else sees it?Only this desk.
  • Since when?This morning, first boot.
  • What changed?The office moved desks over the weekend.

Before you change anything: back up what the desk cannot lose, or verify the user already has.

BPC170 · 1.2
Step 2 · Establish a theory

Commit: what is your leading theory?

Monitor LED amberDesks movedOne PC only

With a partner: Rank your top two suspects before you peek.

Question the obvious first. 1: A video cable came loose in the move. 2: The monitor sits on the wrong input. 3: The video card failed. Cheap and likely leads. Expensive and rare waits.
BPC170 · 1.2
Step 3 · Test the theory

Test one variable at a time.

  1. Test 1 Reseat the video cableStill black
  2. Test 2 Switch monitor input to HDMI 2Logo appears

No theory left?

Escalate when the issue is beyond your skill, beyond your knowledge, or outside company policy and warranty scope.

The obvious theory lost. That is the method working, not failing.

BPC170 · 1.2
Step 4 · Plan and implement

Repair, replace, or work around. Cost decides.

Repair

Fix what you have

#4721: set the input, secure the cable. Parts cost: $0. Chosen.

Replace

Swap the item

When repair costs more than the part, stop repairing.

Work around

Keep work moving

A loaner monitor buys time when the full fix must wait.

BPC170 · 1.2
Step 5 · Verify

Verified means it works and keeps working.

  • Cold boot twice. Full display both times.
  • R. Alvarez signs off at the desk.
  • Preventive step: cable clipped and labeled so the next move cannot repeat this ticket.

Full function, confirmed by the user, plus one measure that blocks the repeat visit.
