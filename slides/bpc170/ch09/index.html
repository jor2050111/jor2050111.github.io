Check: Remote laptop

Commit: get the laptop online through the phone.

With a partner: You are in a remote location. The laptop needs the phone’s cellular connection, and no cable is on hand. Which setting do you enable?

  1. Tethering.
  2. Mobile hotspot.
  3. Bluetooth pairing.
Hotspot. It shares the cellular connection over a peer-to-peer Wi-Fi link. Tethering does the same job over a USB cable, and the scenario ruled cables out.

Check: Lost phone

Commit: a managed phone disappears in the field.

With a partner: The phone carries customer data and it is gone. Which management layer can erase it from here?

  1. MAM wipes it.
  2. MDM wipes it.
  3. Only the carrier can wipe it.
MDM. Device-level management covers networks, hardware use, reset, and remote wipe. MAM reaches application settings and nothing below them.
BPC170 · 9.3
Laptop rules

Open a laptop like evidence: document everything.

  • Small parts, hand tools. Size the driver to the screw.
  • Plastics and form factors crack. Pry gently.
  • Photograph the underside. Tape each screw to its spot.
  • Photograph every stage so cables go back home.
  • First: unplug the AC adapter and pull the battery.

BPC170 · 9.4
Field damage

The field breaks glass, ports, and airflow.

Screen and touch

  • Backlight failurethe picture goes dark.
  • Broken glassit cuts. Replace at the earliest chance.
  • Digitizer damagetouch input dies with the layer.
  • Cursor driftrecalibrate touch, update drivers.

Case and ports

  • Overheatinga cooling pad buys airflow.
  • Liquid damagethe IP scale rates waterproofing.
  • Port damagephysical wear breaks connectors.
  • Dust and moistureexposure corrodes the inside.

The antenna wraps the screen: seat its connectors firmly, suspect interference, and test the connection. Protectors and cases can dull touch.
