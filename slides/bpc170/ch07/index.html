Check: The stuttering call

Commit: calls break up, downloads run fine. Why?

Latency: delayJitter: delay that varies

With a partner: Choose the measure that explains a choppy call on a fast line, and name the setting that protects voice.

Jitter. Voice rides in real time, so delay that keeps changing scrambles the sound even when bandwidth tests fine. QoS protects the call by prioritizing voice traffic.
