BPC170 · B.1.1
Why certify

Certification is proof, not decoration.

  • Confident. Certified employees trust their own answers.
  • Knowledgeable. The credential maps to tested skill.
  • Faster. They reach job proficiency sooner.
  • Reliable. Hiring managers treat the cert as a predictor.
  • Stronger. They perform at a higher level.

BPC170 · Strategy drill
Check: Read like the exam writes

Commit: which answer survives elimination?

A laptop will not join Wi-Fi. Which is the BEST first step?

  1. A Replace the wireless card
  2. B Reinstall the operating system
  3. C Verify airplane mode is off
  4. D Restart the building’s router
C. A and B are expensive guesses. D reaches past your desk. When the stem says BEST, look for the least invasive, most likely step first.
BPC170 · B.1.4
During the exam

Slow reading beats fast guessing.

  • Read for keywordsBEST and MOST mean several answers are partly right.
  • Eliminate firstCross out the obviously wrong before you choose.
  • Flag and returnBank the questions you know, then circle back.
  • Trust your instinctChange an answer only when you can say why it is wrong.
