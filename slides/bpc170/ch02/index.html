Modern video

HDMI

DisplayPort

BPC170 · 2.1
Look-alike ports

Not every oval port is plain USB-C.

Thunderbolt

USB-C shape, 40 Gbps

Versions 3 and 4 move 40 Gbps and daisy chain displays and storage. The bolt icon by the port is the tell.

Lightning

Apple’s own connector

iPhone and iPad for a decade. New devices ship USB-C instead, so adapters keep both alive on your bench.

Same oval, different powers. Read the icon before you promise performance.

SATA power

Molex

BPC170 · 2.2
Before you touch the board

Ground yourself before the board pays for it.

  • Power off and unplugged. Never work energized.
  • Wrist strap on, clipped to the grounded mat.
  • Parts stay in ESD bags until they seat.

A zap you cannot feel can still kill a chip.

Check: The missing standoffs

Two screw holes have nothing under them. Ship it?

Commit first: What happens if the board goes in anyway and power arrives?

The case becomes a short circuit. The unsupported board flexes onto bare steel, traces touch metal, and the board can die at first power. Standoffs are step three, before the board ever enters the case.
