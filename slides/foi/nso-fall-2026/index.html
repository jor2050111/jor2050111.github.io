FOI · Check your thinking
Predict first

Of those six jobs, which one writes the most English in a week?

Commit: Pick one and say it to the person next to you before we look.

All six. Every one of these roles spends more time writing, explaining, and persuading than typing code. The technical half is the half schools teach you. The other half is the one that gets you promoted.
FOI · The pattern
The pattern behind the pay

The best roles sit in the middle.

Builds it Strong technical skill, quiet in the room.
Builds it and explains it Fewer people can do both. This is where the strongest roles sit.
Explains it Great in the room, cannot check the work.

You are going to practice both here. That is deliberate.

FOI · Next steps
Start here

Five things to do in your first eight weeks.

  • Go to office hours in week two, before anything is wrong. Introduce yourself. That is the whole visit.
  • Open a GitHub account this week. Every piece of work you make goes in it.
  • Join the Computer Science Club. The people in that room become your study group first and your network later.
  • Pick one certification target. CompTIA A+ is the usual first one, and it is employable on its own.
  • Keep a file of what you built and one sentence on why you built it that way. You will need it at your first interview.
