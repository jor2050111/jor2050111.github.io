## Design notes

- Palette: `golden-hour`, chosen for a participation and milestone
  session rather than a lecture.
- Motif: trail markers. One trailhead, seven routes, numbered waypoints.
- Timing in `data-notes` adds up to 45 minutes with room for questions.
- Slide 7 uses the shared reveal control for a predict-and-commit check.
- Slide 6 is a made-up illustration, labeled as one on the slide. It
  carries no sourced figures.
