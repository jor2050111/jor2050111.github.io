Quick Check 12.4

Critique it with the court case. Then rewrite it.

A student's Accountability section reads: "The AI tool is responsible for the accuracy of its outputs." Critique it using the court case in this section, and rewrite it for a home lab build checklist.

A tool cannot answer for a result. In Mata v. Avianca, the court put the gatekeeping role on the attorneys who filed, under a rule about signing court papers. Name a person or a role, for example: “I answer for the accuracy of this build checklist. I check every step before I run it.”

CIS137 · Course conclusion
Course conclusion

Five habits of the human in the loop.

  1. Keep a log.The prompt log and the Verification Trail
  2. Verify before you trust.The Output Checklist and the Accuracy Audit
  3. Protect what is not yours to share.The privacy plan, the AI Use Policy, the Ethics Statement’s Privacy section
  4. Choose by criteria.The Requirements Sheet, the Comparison Matrix, the Selection Decision
  5. Know when to say no.The no-AI option and the “no AI here” decision

Mei Lin will walk into the owner's office with a recommendation she can defend, because every line traces to a run someone checked.
