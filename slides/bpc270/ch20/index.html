BPC270 · 20.2
Device and OS issues

Four complaints fill most mobile tickets.

  • OS fails to updateOlder models feel updates hardest, and no rollback exists.Report it, then wait for the vendor fix
  • Random rebootsA stability fault with no single face.Reboot, then patch
  • Slow to respondThe everyday complaint on aging phones.Reboot, then patch
  • Auto-rotate not workingOften a switch, not a fault.Check rotation lock in the quick panel

Reboot clears temporary faults, so it opens almost every one of these tickets.

BPC270 · 20.2
App issues

The tracking app crashed. Work the list, not the panic.

  1. Crash or freeze. Android: Settings > Apps, then Force Stop or Disable. Apple: swipe up from the bottom, then swipe the app card up to close.
  2. Still failing. Clear the cache.
  3. Will not install. Verify storage and permissions. Corporate devices may carry restrictions, and downloads may be limited to Wi-Fi.
