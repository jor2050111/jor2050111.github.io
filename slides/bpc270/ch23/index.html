BPC270 · C.1.4
During the exam

Every strategy is a pacing rule now.

  • Read slowly onceSlower than rereading it three times.
  • Eliminate firstTwo crossed answers turn a guess into a coin with edges.
  • Flag and returnBank the known. The clock pays interest.
  • Trust your instinctChange an answer only when you can say why.
