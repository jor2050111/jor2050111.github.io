Synthetic full: the incrementals append to the end of the original full. One full on the first of the month at 7:00 a.m., then incrementals every 3 hours.

Data destruction and disposal

Reformatting is not forgetting.

Sanitize · the drive survives

  • Erasing and wiping
  • Low level format
  • Secure erase
  • Instant secure erase

Destroy · the drive does not

  • Drilling
  • Shredding
  • Incineration
  • Degaussing magnetic media only
Certificate of destruction or recycling

The paper that proves the data is gone.

Degaussing erases HDDs and magnetic tape. It does nothing to SSDs or optical media.

No. Degaussing only works on magnetic media. It erases HDDs and tape, not SSDs or optical media. Use secure erase or physical destruction, and file the certificate of destruction.
