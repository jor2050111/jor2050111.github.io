    What does this little model leave out?

    Cooling depends on moving heat off the CPU and out of the case. The model uses the lower of its CPU-cooler capacity and case-airflow capacity. Higher workloads ask for more cooling. If demand exceeds capacity, the model shows reduced pace.

    These are invented comparison units, not degrees, watts, benchmark results, or buying advice. The paths are diagrams, not fluid simulations. Real cases differ. Passive vents still exchange air, and an all-intake or all-exhaust setup does not seal the case.

    Fan speed affects case fans here. Each CPU cooler includes its own working fan and correctly applied thermal interface. Dust, room temperature, fan curves, GPU cooling, and case geometry are held constant. No thermal paste or liquid-cooling installation is simulated.

    Chapter concepts: intake and exhaust, a clear air path, and the CPU-to-case cooling path. Technical reference: Intel’s PC cooling guide (opens in a new tab).
