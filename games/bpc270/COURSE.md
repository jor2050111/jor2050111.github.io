## Course and scope

BPC270: A+ Exam Prep: Operating System Configuration and Support. The course
covers operating systems, software troubleshooting, security, and operational
procedures. Optional games complement the existing lessons and practice.
Use two or three chapter concepts, individual untimed play by default, no
grades or required account, and a provisional first play under eight minutes.

The [local slide index](../../slides/bpc270/index.html) states that BPC270
begins with shared Module 1 in BPC170, then continues at Chapter 11. Do not
renumber Chapter 11 to Chapter 01 or manufacture Chapters 02–10 for this course.
Canvas module numbers require a separate check when a Canvas task is requested.

## Creative play and course sequence

Use the shared [creative-play standard](../educational-game-builder/references/creative-play.md).
Do not default to an OS settings dashboard, installation wizard, help-desk
checklist, or security quiz with rewards. Read the selected chapter's existing
practice, then explain what distinct choices and consequences the game adds.
Compare three families, including two settings beyond a literal bench or help
desk. Explain any analogy's limits and support different viable approaches.

Read the [shared catalog](../GAME-CATALOG.md) and inspect course folders on
each request. BPC270 has no games or reserved mechanics as of this setup.
Its sequence is independent of BPC170. No first family is selected. BPC170's
Premiere Night, The Last Copy, and Mirrorfall remain that course's unassigned
ideas, not BPC270 commitments. Do not clone an existing game's world or layout.

Ask for reference images before generating or drawing Phoenix, Arizona, or
Phoenix College scenes, as required by the shared workflow.
