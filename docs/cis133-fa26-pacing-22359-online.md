# CIS133 Pacing Guide — Class 22359 (Online)

**Fall 2026 · Phoenix College · Online Canvas · J. Vega**
**Class dates:** Sep 8 – Dec 13, 2026 (14 weeks, late start)

## How this guide is built

| Constraint | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Starts Tue Sep 8 (day after Labor Day) | Week 1 is a six-day week |
| Week 14 reserved for finals | No new content |
| Twelve chapters, fourteen weeks | Only one double-week is affordable |

A strict reading of "week 1 is orientation only" leaves exactly twelve teaching
weeks for twelve chapters and **no double-weeks at all**. That also drops
Chapter 11 — one of the densest chapters in the book — onto Thanksgiving week.

This guide makes one change to avoid that: **Module 0 shares Week 1 with
Chapter 1.** Orientation in an asynchronous section is a notification check, a
syllabus review, an intro post, and a readiness quiz — one to two hours.
Chapter 1 is the only chapter with no build work. They fit together.

The freed week goes to **Chapter 12**, which spans Thanksgiving and carries the
capstone. That placement matters more than it looks: it puts the reading-heavy,
low-stakes half of Chapter 12 on the holiday week and gives the capstone a full
clean week to publish and verify.

## Calendar hazards

| Week | Date | Impact |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Week by week

### Week 1 · Sep 8–13 · Module 0 + Chapter 1

**Module 0:** Canvas notification settings, platform access, syllabus and
policy review, academic integrity and AI use, intro discussion.

**Chapter 1 (§1.1–1.4):** Internet vs. web, client/server, the request path,
URL anatomy and DNS, search and source evaluation, ethics and privacy.

**Due Sun Sep 13:** 0.5 Intro discussion · 0.6 Readiness & Syllabus Review ·
Quick Checks 1.1–1.4 · Skills Lab 1A

Chapter 1 carries three CLOs (I, II, III), and Lab 1A is the only place CLO III
is primarily assessed. Do not let the orientation tasks crowd out the
source-evaluation part of that lab.

### Week 2 · Sep 14–20 · Chapter 2 — Basic HTML Elements

VS Code and the save-refresh loop, the document skeleton, headings,
paragraphs, lists, links, and the W3C validator.

**Due Sun Sep 20:** Quick Checks 2.1–2.4 · Skills Lab 2A

Post the workspace setup walkthrough in Week 1 so install problems surface
before this module opens.

### Week 3 · Sep 21–27 · Chapter 3 — Semantic HTML and Images

Semantic vs. non-semantic elements, the four landmarks, `img` attributes, alt
text by the image's job, raster vs. vector, folder structure.

**Due Sun Sep 27:** Skills Lab 3A · Discussion 3.7 "Writing for Robots?"

### Week 4 · Sep 28–Oct 4 · Chapter 4 — CSS Foundations ⚠️ heaviest week

Rule anatomy, the three connection methods, the cascade, element/class/id
selectors, color systems, the CSS validator.

**Midweek gate — due Wed Sep 30:** Quick Checks 4.1–4.2 · **Lab 4A Part 1**
**Due Sun Oct 4:** Quick Checks 4.3–4.4 · Lab 4A Parts 2–3 + Q&A

This is the chapter I would most want to split, and the calendar will not pay
for it. Use a **midweek checkpoint** instead: gate §4.1–4.2 and Lab Part 1 on
Wednesday so students cannot postpone the whole chapter to the weekend. It is
the longest chapter in the book (7,693 words, 20 code blocks) and the point
where students go from one language to two.

Run no discussion this week.

### Week 5 · Oct 5–11 · Chapter 5 — The Box Model and Typography

Four nested layers, the DevTools box diagram, shorthand spacing, `box-sizing`,
font stacks, readable type.

**Due Sun Oct 11:** Skills Lab 5A · Discussion 5.7 "The Beige Internet"

Second in code volume (19 blocks) and the only chapter that teaches
`box-sizing`.

### Week 6 · Oct 12–18 · Chapter 6 — Layout with Flexbox

Container and item roles, main and cross axes, `justify-content`,
`align-items`, `gap`, `flex-wrap`, combinators, `:hover` and `:focus`, the
navigation bar.

**Due Sun Oct 18:** Skills Lab 6A

### Week 7 · Oct 19–25 · Chapter 7 — UX and Web Design 📌 milestone

The plan-first arithmetic, UX vs. UI, the five principles, sitemaps,
wireframes, navigation labels.

**Due Sun Oct 25:** Skills Lab 7A · Discussion 7.7 "Skip the Sketch?" ·
**Project milestone: site plan**

### Week 8 · Oct 26–Nov 1 · Chapter 8 — Responsive Design and Media Queries

The viewport meta tag, DevTools device mode, query anatomy, content-driven
breakpoints, base styles plus overrides.

**Due Sun Nov 1:** Skills Lab 8A

### Week 9 · Nov 2–8 · Chapter 9 — Web Accessibility ⚠️ second heaviest

WCAG, POUR, conformance levels, heading order, landmarks, link text, the
keyboard walk, contrast math, `rem` sizing, alt-text decisions, the evaluator
and human checks, finding triage.

**Midweek gate — due Wed Nov 4:** Quick Checks 9.1–9.2 · **Lab 9A Part 1**
**Due Sun Nov 8:** Quick Checks 9.3–9.4 · Lab 9A Parts 2–3 + Q&A ·
Discussion 9.7 "The Million-Dollar Widget"

Same treatment as Chapter 4. This chapter has the most Try It Yourself
exercises in the book (six) and the longest Skills Lab, and its two halves are
genuinely different work: structural repair before the gate, perceptual rules
and the audit after.

### Week 10 · Nov 9–15 · Chapter 10 — HTML Tables 📌 milestone

The two-axes test, rows and header cells, caption and `scope`, zebra striping,
the phone overflow wrapper.

**Due Sun Nov 15:** Skills Lab 10A (events page) · **Project milestone: draft pages**

### Week 11 · Nov 16–22 · Chapter 11 — HTML Forms

The label/id contract, the input family, radio groups sharing one `name`,
fieldset and legend, name-value pairs, GET and POST, the styled accessible
form.

**Due Sun Nov 22:** Skills Lab 11A · Discussion 11.7 "The Last Web Developer?"

Keeping this chapter off Thanksgiving week is the whole reason Module 0 shares
Week 1. Section 11.2 alone teaches seven controls.

### Week 12 · Nov 23–29 · Chapter 12, Part 1 — §12.1–12.2 ⚠️ Thanksgiving

The third layer and the `script` element, the live footer year, the client and
server halves, "help is not security," static sites and the HTTPS law.

**Due Sun Nov 29:** Quick Checks 12.1–12.2 · **Lab 12A Part 1** — six pages
assembled and validating to zero

Reading-heavy, low-stakes, no new syntax to debug alone over a holiday. Open
this module early — Fri Nov 20 — so students who travel can work ahead.

### Week 13 · Nov 30–Dec 6 · Chapter 12, Part 2 — §12.3–12.4 🎓 capstone

Hosting, the secure transfer family, why relative paths survive the move,
`index.html`, the eight-check pre-flight, publishing and live verification.

**Due Sun Dec 6:** Lab 12A Parts 2–3 + Q&A · **Capstone site live**

Set an earlier soft deadline — **publish by Thu Dec 3** — so the weekend is
available for fixing the relative paths that break on first upload. That
failure is predictable; build time for it into the schedule instead of the
inbox.

### Week 14 · Dec 7–13 · Finals

## If you must keep Week 1 as orientation only

Then it is twelve chapters in twelve weeks, one per week, no doubles, and
Chapter 11 falls on Thanksgiving week (Nov 23–29) with the capstone in the
final teaching week. It works, but every chapter gets the same weight
regardless of its density, and the two hardest weeks land where students have
the least support. The midweek-gate technique above becomes essential rather
than optional for Chapters 4, 9, and 11.
