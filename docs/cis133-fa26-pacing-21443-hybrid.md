**Fall 2026 · Phoenix College · E224 · M/W 11:00–11:50 AM · J. Vega**
**Class dates:** Aug 24 – Dec 18, 2026 (17 weeks)

## How this guide is built

| Constraint | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Week 1 reserved for orientation | Module 0 only |
| Week 17 reserved for finals | No new content |
| Remaining | **15 teaching weeks for 12 chapters** |

Fifteen weeks for twelve chapters buys three double-weeks. They go to
**Chapter 4**, **Chapter 9**, and **Chapter 12** — the two chapters with the
highest concept density and the one that carries the capstone.

Seat time is only 100 minutes a week across two 50-minute meetings, so the
live sessions cannot cover a chapter. Each meeting below names the one thing
worth doing in the room. Everything else belongs to the online half.

## Calendar hazards

This schedule has **zero slack**. If you want a catch-up week, drop the
Chapter 12 split and make Week 14 a flex week (see *Alternative* at the end).

### Week 17 · Dec 14–18 · Finals

## Alternative: trade the Chapter 12 split for a flex week

If you would rather carry a buffer than double Chapter 12, drop that split and
make **Week 14 (Nov 23–29) a catch-up and capstone-work week with no new
chapter**. Chapters 11 and 12 then land in Weeks 15 and 16 on clean full weeks,
Thanksgiving costs you nothing, and any week that ran long earlier has
somewhere to go. The cost is that the capstone publishes in the final teaching
week with no runway.

Take the buffer if this is your first run of the 12-chapter book. Take the
Chapter 12 split if you have run it before and know the pace holds.
